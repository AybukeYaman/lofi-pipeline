## Faz 1 — Master frame

**Araç:** Google AI Studio, Gemini image (Nano Banana)
**Ekler:** Image 1 = kompozisyon referansı (sahne), Image 2 = stil görseli
**Çıktı:** `resimler/MASTER.png`

```text
Image 1 = environment / composition reference.
Image 2 = visual style reference.

Recreate the scene from Image 1 in the exact visual style of Image 2, guided by the Style Block below.

KEEP from Image 1:
* subject identity
* environment / scene
* object placement
* framing
* layout
* spatial relationships
* camera angle

TAKE from Image 2 and the Style Block:
* rendering technique
* color palette
* lighting
* line quality
* texture / grain
* contrast
* shading

Output a single still image. Do not change the camera angle.

Style description:
{{STYLE_BLOCK}}
```
